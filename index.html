Use the morph as your camera on OmeTV, Discord, Zoom, etc.

A website can't turn itself into a webcam, so you use the free program OBS Studio to pass this window along as a "virtual camera". One-time setup, about 5 minutes:

  1. Download and install OBS Studio from obsproject.com.
  2. Here, click Webcam (live), pick your settings, then click Clean screen for OBS. Only your morphed face shows now. (Press Esc to get the controls back.)
  3. In OBS, under Sources click + → Window Capture (Mac: macOS Screen Capture → Window) and choose this browser window. Right-click the source → Transform → Fit to screen.
  4. In OBS click Start Virtual Camera (bottom right).
  5. On OmeTV / Discord / Zoom, open the camera setting and choose OBS Virtual Camera.

Turn Mirror off before you start so text and your movements look normal to the other person. Keep this browser window open (it can be behind other windows, just not minimized).
